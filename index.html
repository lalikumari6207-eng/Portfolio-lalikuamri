Who I Am

About Me

I am Lali Kumari, Full Stack Developer and Founder & CEO of Lyzonix Technologies.

I am from Bihar, India, and I am pursuing a B.Sc in Chemistry — a background that gave me strong analytical thinking and an eye for detail I now apply to building products.

I am a visionary entrepreneur focused on building scalable digital solutions. I don't just follow trends — I create them. With a strong foundation in technology and business knowledge, I develop innovative IT solutions that help businesses grow faster and smarter.

I believe in execution over ideas, consistency over shortcuts, and building something that creates real value. My mission is to establish Lyzonix as a powerful multi-service company.

I am also an author. I am not a graduate in this field, but what I feel, what I explore and what I went through in my struggle phase became my writing. I have written my own book, Mindful Roots, Resilient Wings, and I continue to write more.

I don't just follow trends — I create them.
